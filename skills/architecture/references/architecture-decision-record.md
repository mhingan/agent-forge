# Architecture Decision Record

Use one ADR for a decision with meaningful long-term impact. Keep it concise enough to review and detailed enough to understand years later.

## Context

Describe the problem, business outcome, constraints, existing architecture, workload assumptions, quality attributes, and why a decision is needed now. Separate verified facts from assumptions.

## Decision drivers

Rank the drivers that matter: correctness, security, privacy, availability, latency, scale, cost, delivery time, operability, team familiarity, reversibility, and compatibility.
