## Threat scenarios
For each scenario: attacker capability, preconditions, attack path, affected asset, impact, detection, mitigation, and residual risk.

## Abuse cases to consider
- Unauthorized object access / tenant crossover
- Privilege escalation
- Prompt injection through retrieved or tool-sourced content
- Credential theft or accidental disclosure
- SSRF and unsafe URL fetches
- Injection and unsafe deserialization
- Resource exhaustion and abuse of expensive endpoints
- Replay, duplicate processing, and race conditions
